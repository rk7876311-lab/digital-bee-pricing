FAQ
Common Questions
How long does it take to build an app?
A basic MVP takes 4–6 weeks. A full-featured growth app takes 8–12 weeks. Enterprise apps with custom architecture take 16–24 weeks. Timelines depend on the scope and complexity of your project.
Do you build for both iOS and Android?
Yes. We use React Native and Flutter to build cross-platform apps that run natively on both iOS and Android from a single codebase, reducing cost and timeline. For enterprise clients, we also build native apps separately.
Who owns the code after the project?
You do. 100%. Upon final payment, full source code ownership, all assets, API keys, and repository access are transferred to you. No lock-in, ever.
What is your payment structure?
We work with a milestone-based payment model: 40% to begin, 40% at design completion, and 20% on final delivery. This protects both parties and keeps things transparent.
Can you work with my existing backend or APIs?
Absolutely. We integrate seamlessly with your existing systems. Provide us your API documentation and we'll work with whatever stack you have — or build a new one if needed.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes. After your launch support period, we offer monthly maintenance retainers starting at ₹12,000/month covering bug fixes, OS updates, feature additions, and performance monitoring.
